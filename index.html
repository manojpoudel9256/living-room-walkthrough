Nepal living room

Walk in from the entrance passage and look around.

  • WASD move
  • Mouse look
  • Shift walk faster
  • Click/E use what you look at: wall switches, AC remote, doors
  • F both fans on / off
  • Esc pause
  • Left thumb: move
  • Right side: drag to look
  • Tap switches, the AC remote or doors
  • Bulb (top right): lights, fans, AC · right edge: landscape, door, fans

Provisional design: only the 12 × 16 ft room and the 10 ft ceiling are confirmed measurements.
