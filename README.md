# Living room walkthrough

A first-person three.js walkthrough of a provisional 12 × 16 ft living-room design, with lighting baked in Blender Cycles.

**Open it:** https://manojpoudel9256.github.io/living-room-walkthrough/

- Desktop: click *Enter the room*, then WASD to move and the mouse to look (drag to look if mouse capture is blocked). **E** opens or closes doors, **F** switches the fans on or off.
- Phone: left joystick to move, drag to look.
